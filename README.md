### Work and projects

**Investigative and AI evaluation studies — private review stage**

I'm developing small, reproducible exercises to explore how technical findings can be challenged and verified:

- **Evidence Integrity Lab** — a synthetic SHA-256 file-inventory checker with positive and negative tests. It demonstrates change detection *and* the limitations of unauthenticated manifests.
- **AI Evaluation Laboratory** — an offline, rule-based exercise for testing fictional structured analytical responses against specified source and claim requirements. It is **not** a live model benchmark or a semantic truth evaluator.
- **All-source analysis exercise** — a fictional assessment comparing competing hypotheses, source limitations and confidence judgments.

These are **AI-assisted educational drafts**, not operational tools or employer-endorsed work. They remain unpublished while I review their code, provenance and technical claims. Results from synthetic tests should not be confused with real-world field validation.

### How I approach technical work

**Evidence before assumption.** Preserve source context, check alternative explanations, and make uncertainty explicit.

**Verification before claims.** Favor repeatable tests, negative scenarios and documented limitations over impressive-looking results.

**Human accountability.** Analytical judgment and investigative responsibility cannot be outsourced to a tool.
